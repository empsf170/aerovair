Our Story

A Legacy of Excellence

Since our inaugural flight in 1995, AEROVAIR has been driven by a singular vision: to make air travel not just a means of transport, but a luxurious and memorable part of the journey itself.

Today, we connect over 120 destinations across 6 continents, employing over 20,000 dedicated professionals who share our passion for premium hospitality and aviation innovation.
